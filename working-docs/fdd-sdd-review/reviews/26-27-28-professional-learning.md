## Summary

This review covers the Sponsor-, Educator-, and Processing-facing Professional Learning FDDs — the
three folders FDD 14's admin review flagged as "not yet reviewed" and cross-referenced for the
domain's two most consequential open items. Both cross-domain checks came back negative: none of
FDD 26, 27, 28, or the superseded processing draft describe IDP content, creation, or approval
mechanics, and none mention "College Conversion" or corroborate its credits × 25 SCECH formula —
the silence is total on both counts, which strengthens rather than resolves `client-questions.md`
#9 and leaves the Discrepancy #2 finding from the FDD 14 review as a still-unconfirmed,
lowest-formality-source-only claim. Independently, this review's own text comparison surfaced a
new, load-bearing discrepancy: `proflearning-domain.md`'s Sponsor aggregate explicitly states
"there is no PendingApproval state" for sponsors (EEM-only creation), but FDD 26's Addendum
describes a full new-Sponsor application form with a Lead Administrator e-signature gate — a
Sponsor-side onboarding workflow the domain doesn't model at all, contradicting its own
`proflearning.sponsor.approve` permission. A second real discrepancy: FDD 27 describes an
educator-initiated SCECH correction/dispute pathway that bypasses the sponsor/coordinator
entirely, but `SCECHCorrectionRequest` is modeled exclusively as sponsor-initiated. Processing (28)
mostly confirms the existing admin worklist/attendance model but exposes two more items needing a
decision (admin edits bypassing re-approval; a possible general admin credit-override capability
outside the formal correction-request path) and several coverage gaps (missing sequences the
domain doc itself already cites, missing Sponsor CRUD fields, and corroborating evidence for the
FDD 14 review's tentative "New Teacher PD" gap). The `TBDeleted_` processing draft triages the same
way FDD 14's `Old-` subtree did: same narrative, shorter/earlier, superseded by the current FDD 28 —
with one exception worth preserving (see Coverage Gap #4).

  `Old-`-prefixed folders (FDD 14) and an Addendum-retracted feature (FDD 13/14). It triaged the

  worthless once corroborated elsewhere.

  task specifically asked this review to check FDD 26/27/28 for IDP and "College Conversion"
  evidence, and the honest answer for both was "still nothing." Reporting a clean negative (rather
  than stretching thin evidence to manufacture a resolution) is the correct outcome per the
  judiciousness guidance in `client-questions.md`, and it lets that row's "Why It Matters" note
  reflect that three separate FDD folders (14, and now 26/27/28) have all been checked and found
  silent — useful context for whoever eventually asks the client.
