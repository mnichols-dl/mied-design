## Summary

These 8 documents describe the application, eligibility, approval-routing, and renewal rules for Michigan's seven temporary/substitute educator permit types (Daily Substitute, Extended Daily, Emergency Extended Daily, Full Year Basic Substitute, Full Year Shortage Substitute, School Administrator, School Social Worker) plus a dedicated cross-cutting Renewals document. The business-rule substance — permit duration caps (90/180/270 days), renewal limits (3 for FYBSP/FYSS, 1 for SSW, 0 for Daily/Extended/Emergency Extended/School Administrator), mentor-assignment requirements, and the "one permit fee per school year" rule — matches `credentialing-domain.md`'s "Permit Duration and Renewal Limits," "Mentor Assignment Requirement for Permits," and "Payment Requirement" business rules almost exactly; this is the strongest part of the alignment, and the **Renewals** document in particular reads as though it were written directly from (or informed) those same SDD business rules. Two systemic problems keep every document from a clean "Aligned" status. First, all 8 documents describe permit applications as submitted exclusively by a "School district, ISD, or Staffing Agency user" *on behalf of* the educator — the FDD's own stated Assumption is "Temporary Credentials are applied for by a district or ISD on behalf of an individual" — while `credentialing-sequences.md`'s "Submit Permit Application" and "Renew Credential" sequences show the **educator themselves** as the actor, and `credentialing-permissions.md`'s `credentialing.application.submit` permission is scoped **Self-only**. Second, the FDDs' three-actor-type model ("School district, ISD, or **Staffing Agency**") has no counterpart in the organizations domain: `organizations-capability.md`'s `OrganizationType` enum is `Building|District|ISD|EPP|Nonpublic|PLS` — there is no Staffing Agency type. Beyond these two systemic issues, this review also surfaces a genuine FDD-vs-itself internal inconsistency (the Extended vs. Emergency Extended Daily Substitute permit's prerequisite-permit logic contradicts itself within the same document), and several substantive Coverage Gaps: an entirely unmodeled "Full Year Expert" permit type referenced in all 7 permit-selection screens, no `academic_year` concept anywhere in the credentialing data model despite it being the temporal backbone of every eligibility/expiration rule in the FDDs, no representation of a permit being shared across multiple districts, and no modeling of the two-stage "Needs Responses" / Professional Practice self-disclosure flow that sits in front of (and is distinct from) the externally-sourced `ProfessionalPracticeStatus` the SDD already models.
