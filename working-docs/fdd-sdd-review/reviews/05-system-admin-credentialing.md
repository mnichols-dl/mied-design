## Summary

This FDD covers the "System Administrator – Credentialing" / "Credential Admin" role's master-data and operational-tooling surface: managing Certificate/Permit type definitions (5.1) and Endorsement Classifications/Codes (5.2) — both of which are well modeled in the SDD — plus five features with **no SDD representation at all**: Global Parameters/"Credentialing Controls" (5.3), Batch Jobs (5.4), individual-level Worklist/Report Assignment (5.5), and a long tail of admin capabilities in the unnumbered back half of the Business Specifications (alerts/notifications management, email template management, document-upload/file-queue configuration, reports administration, Question Set/decision-tree configuration, and Internal Comment configuration). Most consequentially for the live design thread this review was asked to inform: **this document — the single most likely place in the entire FDD corpus for an admin "emergency issuance" or "bypass payment/PPR/review" capability to live, since it is explicitly the System-Administrator/config-tooling FDD — describes no such capability anywhere.** It doesn't mention manual/corrective credential issuance, nullification, or any workflow bypass at all; its admin actions are scoped entirely to type-level *definitions* (certificate/permit/endorsement/assessment catalogs) and system configuration, never to instance-level educator credential records. This is strong corroborating evidence for `client-questions.md` #8, which has been updated with this finding rather than duplicated.
