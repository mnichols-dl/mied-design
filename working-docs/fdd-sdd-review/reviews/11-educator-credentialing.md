## Summary

The main `11,16` document is the combined narrative for new-certificate/permit application submission, eligibility evaluation, PPR-gated auto-approval, worklist-based manual review, and OEE/Credential Administrator processing (suspend/revoke/nullify, manual certificate add, comments). It is the best-aligned FDD reviewed in the credentialing family so far: its PPR-gate language (PPR flag hard-exclusion at search, "Needs Responses" flag, "PPR Hold" application status keyed off an account-level "PPR Admin Flag") matches `credentialing-sequences.md`'s just-updated four-way PPR branching (`Clear`/`ConditionalClearance`/`Hold`/`Blocked`) cleanly, and its dual-actor submission model (Citizen/educator self-service alongside a Business Credential Applications role submitting on the educator's behalf, plus a narrower Credential-Administrator "add certificate manually" exception) resolves the open submission-actor question left by the FDD-17 review — see the two priority findings below. The remaining issues are narrower: several worklist/role names in the FDD (a redacted "SOM Credentialing ______" role placeholder, "Educator Teaching Applications" vs. the SDD's flatter application-review permission model) don't yet have a clean SDD counterpart, permit/certificate application numbering has a documented client decision (`TXXXXXX`/`CXXXXXX` prefix scheme) that the domain's ERD doesn't reflect, and the two "Full Year Expert"/"Career Authorization"/"Special Education" credential types referenced throughout (consistent with the still-open FDD-17 questions) recur here as well. The MTTC EdReports (Pearson) interface design document is, as expected, a Boundary Violation — a fixed-width file-layout specification for an external vendor feed, no different in kind from the MiLogin IDDs flagged in the FDD-01 review.
